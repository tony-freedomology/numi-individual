Is this just an AI chat bot?

No. Numi is a discipleship companion with relational memory. It doesn’t just answer questions; it remembers your prayer requests, your struggles, and your growth edges to follow up with you specifically.

Will this replace my church community?

Never. Numi is designed to push you TOWARD community. It will often nudge you to reach out to a friend, bring something up in your small group, or talk to your pastor about what you’re learning.

Is my personal data safe?

Yes. Your conversations with Numi are private and encrypted. We don’t sell your data, and we don’t share your individual prayers or struggles with anyone else.

Do I have to download an app?

No. Numi lives entirely in your native text messaging app. No new passwords to remember, no notifications to manage. Just simple, life-giving connection where you already are.

What Numi is not

It isn’t a chatbot, a replacement for church, or a Bible search engine — it’s a companion that knows you.
